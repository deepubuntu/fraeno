Privacy

Last updated August 10, 2026

What we collect

When you submit the access request form, we receive the name, email address, optional company, and message you type. The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts.

How it is used

Your submission is delivered as one email to DeepUbuntu Labs and is used only to reply to you about Fraeno. It is not sold, shared, or added to any marketing list.

Where it lives

Submissions are transmitted over HTTPS, relayed by Cloudflare, and stored in our email inbox. No separate database of submissions is kept.

Your choices

To have your messages deleted, email thabhelo@deepubuntu.com and we will remove them.
